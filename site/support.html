How can we help?

Before you begin

Tutaua requires access to a working Jellyfin server. It does not include a subscription or media catalogue.

Connect to your server

Enter the complete address of your Jellyfin server, including https:// and any required port. Publicly accessible servers should use HTTPS. A private IP address using HTTP, such as http://192.168.1.20:8096, can be used on your home network.

Sign-in issues

Confirm the server address in a browser and check your username and password. Your Jellyfin administrator can verify that your account is enabled and allowed to access the selected libraries.

Playback issues

Try the same title in another Jellyfin client. If it also fails, check the server logs and media permissions. Some formats require the Jellyfin server to transcode; hardware acceleration and available server resources can affect playback quality.

Clear your session

Sign out from Tutaua to remove the active session. You can also clear the app data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored Tutaua data.

Contact

For assistance, contact @yukista on GitHub. Please do not include your password or access token in a support request.

Independent software

Tutaua is an independent client and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.
